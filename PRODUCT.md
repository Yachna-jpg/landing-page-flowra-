# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

High-volume lead teams and growth businesses across India (Real estate developers, EdTech institutes, D2C/e-commerce brands, healthcare clinics, event organizers, and performance marketing agencies) whose leads decay when sales reps take hours to follow up.

## Product Purpose

Flowra is an autonomous revenue conversation operating layer. It captures inbound signals from Meta ads, web forms, and WhatsApp, qualifies leads in under 6 seconds through knowledge-grounded WhatsApp chat and native Indian voice calls (Hindi, Hinglish, regional dialects), syncs pipeline data to CRMs, and escalates hot threads to human sales reps with complete context intact.

## Positioning

The only platform in India unifying official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API chat, sub-400ms low-latency Hinglish voice calling, and deterministic knowledge grounding—delivering measurable speed-to-lead instead of fragmented chatbots and ungrounded AI hallucinations.

## Operating Context

- Shared multi-agent team inbox with unassigned/needs-reply filters
- Visual drag-and-drop workflow builder (if/else intent branches)
- Meta Lead Ads webhook and website chat widget integration
- Call recordings, transcripts, and lead quality scoring
- Real-time bi-directional CRM stages (HubSpot, LeadSquared, Zoho, Google Sheets)

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**: Verified business onboarding, permanent green-tick readiness, number retention.
- **Native Regional Voice Calling**: Multi-accent conversational models (Hinglish, Delhi Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi) with sub-400ms latency.
- **Grounded Knowledge Base**: Ingestion of PDFs, websites, catalogues, and sheets into a vector index (56+ chunks) with zero pricing hallucinations.
- **Human Handoff**: Immediate automation pause upon human intervention; lead assigned with full conversation history attached.
- **Speed to Lead**: Under 6-second first touch response.

## Product Principles

1. **Grounded Over Clever**: The assistant speaks only from verified source material; it gracefully escalates to a human rather than hallucinating answers.
2. **Speed to Lead Wins**: Respond in seconds on WhatsApp and voice while the lead's intent is hot.
3. **Hardware-Grade Craft**: Clean interfaces with intentional contrast, readable data tables, and zero generic AI slop.
4. **Seamless Human Parity**: AI qualifies and assists; the sales rep closes with full context.
